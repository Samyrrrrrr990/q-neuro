# Roadmap

## v0.1 — Foundational synthetic evidence (complete)

- Causal NeuroWorld, explicit missingness, chronology counterfactuals, ambiguity, composition,
  omitted disease, hidden syndrome, and active acquisition.
- Conventional and hypothesis-state architecture suites with matched budgets where practical.
- Multi-world replication, critical ablations, observable probes, training laws, realized halting,
  actual state trajectories, and registered discovery queue.
- Reproducible artifacts, analyses, figures, dashboard, manuscript, claim ledger, and failure log.

## v0.2 — Independent-simulator replication

- Implement the fixed two-state attractor and ambiguity-aware measurement proposals.
- Add at least one independently designed causal generator with different factorization and token
  semantics.
- Tune conventional controls with equal validation budgets; increase world and training seeds.
- Predefine leakage tests and a statistical analysis plan before the first outcome is observed.

## v0.3 — Scaling and efficiency

- Run 0.1M–3M parameter families only if v0.2 preserves the robustness effect.
- Add hardware-level energy and FLOP estimates and peak-memory profiling in isolated processes.
- Test whether evidence-conditioned sparsity realizes compute savings without accuracy selection
  on test data.

## External-data gate

No clinical or public medical dataset work begins until licensing, governance, intended-use,
de-identification, bias, and evaluation protocols are documented. Any external study starts as a
non-deployment retrospective research analysis and requires domain-expert collaboration.
