Released 14 August 2026.

## Evidence boundary

The release falsifies intrinsic complex-arithmetic robustness only for the implemented operator and
executed synthetic profiles. It does not establish universal superiority of real networks, cover
every nonlinear complex model, or support medical claims. The reduced discovery and held-out
studies are outcome-ineligible for the deferred grand protocol, and no independent team has yet
replicated the work.
