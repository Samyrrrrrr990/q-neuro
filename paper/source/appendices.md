# Extended methods and supplementary results

## A. Preregistration hierarchy

The next-phase preregistration distinguishes exploratory smoke tests, reduced discovery, provisional held-out confirmation, and QN-GRAND-001. Only the grand protocol can support its strongest outcome category. Result directories carry the preregistration identifier, hypothesis identifier, configuration, environment, and status. Smoke profiles explicitly state that they are not evidence.

The minimum practical favorable effect is +0.02 top-1. A complex advantage would require a positive aggregate estimate whose adjusted interval clears that value against the full real envelope. An equivalence or negative result against the exact real block adjudicates the intrinsic-arithmetic claim separately from performance against other real architectures.

## B. Discovery configuration

Discovery families were analytic commutative, analytic noncommutative, hidden causal machine, and sequential detective. The source world seed was 81001. Training sizes were 250 and 1,000; validation used 300 cases; each shifted test world used 120 cases; and each counterfactual set used 40 pairs. Twenty-four world seeds and severities 0.0, 0.5, and 1.0 were crossed with five training seeds.

The five evaluated models were complex operator, exact-real-block operator, structured state space, GRU, and real polar operator. Each used the same nominal parameter budget of 20,304, rank 2 for operator models, sequence limit 96, batch size 128, learning rate 0.001, weight decay 0.0001, six epochs, and patience three. Discovery produced 200 training runs, 14,400 evaluation records, and 2,880 paired effects.

## C. Held-out configuration

Held-out families were Bayesian urn, hidden-rule relational, machine-fault diagnosis, and network-intrusion reasoning. Their order-dependence controls were 0.0, 1.0, 0.3, and 0.75, respectively. The source world seed was 91001. Training size was 1,000, with 300 validation cases, 120 cases per shifted world, 40 counterfactual pairs, 32 new world seeds, and five new training seeds.

Architecture and optimization settings were unchanged. The study produced 100 training runs, 9,600 evaluation records, 1,920 paired effects, and 12 family-by-severity law aggregates. The four families, all seeds, and the confirmation configuration were stored in the frozen artifact before execution.

## E. Hierarchical analysis

The held-out hierarchical estimate is computed from 640 family-world-seed observations after averaging lower-level severity effects as specified by the analysis. Resampling gives equal top-level weight to the four generator families and resamples worlds and seed observations within family. Twenty thousand resamples yield an estimate of -0.009158 and a 95% interval from -0.013251 to -0.004570.

The nested distribution has n = 1,920, median 0, standard deviation 0.02355, probability of superiority 0, trimmed mean -0.002485, and worst-decile mean -0.04167. The world distribution has n = 128, median -0.008056, standard deviation 0.007361, trimmed mean -0.008397, and worst-decile mean -0.01944.

## F. Grand-test readiness decision

The QN-GRAND-001 preflight passed eight checks and failed six. Failed gates were:

- Complete the 14-model real comparator envelope; only four required real controls were evaluated.
- Execute equal or real-favoring search; observed maximum trials were one per evaluated model.
- Preserve per-trial FLOPs and optimizer steps.
- Execute the full ShiftGauntlet model-outcome grid.
- Complete the full rather than reduced discovery protocol.
- Preserve raw prediction artifacts.

The preflight status is `blocked_before_execution`. The benchmark-access flag is false, the execution flag is false, and no primary effect exists. This artifact is single-use and was not rerun after a documentation amendment.

## G. Reproducibility and artifact map

The evidence layer contains immutable experiment result directories, a SQLite registry, normalized analysis JSON, the frozen law JSON, a machine-readable claim ledger, a failure ledger, generated PNG and PDF figures, the manuscript source, and a browser-based dashboard. A release verifier checks artifact presence, hashes, claim audit status, QN-GRAND-001 seal status, and the repository test suite. Cached-artifact reproduction does not require rerunning thousands of training evaluations.

## H. Reporting checklist

- Every effect names its comparator.
- Discovery, held-out reduced confirmation, and grand confirmation are not conflated.
- Exact functional equivalence is separated from optimization and architecture hypotheses.
- Failed thresholds and blocked gates are reported with passed checks.
- No external dataset or patient evidence is implied.
- No priority, clinical, universal-superiority, or physical-quantum claim is made.
