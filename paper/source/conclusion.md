# Conclusion

An apparent complex-valued robustness advantage does not survive an exact real control. The Q-Neuro complex operator and its tied real block produce the same held-out predictions at numerical precision, and the executed best-real envelope yields zero positive complex effects in 2,880 discovery and 1,920 held-out cells. A frozen quadratic candidate fits discovery but fails held-out magnitude prediction. The preregistered grand benchmark remains sealed because six readiness gates are unmet.

These findings falsify an intrinsic complex-arithmetic interpretation within the tested synthetic scope. They do not establish a universal ranking of complex and real networks, and they provide no clinical or quantum-mechanistic evidence. The durable contribution is methodological: preserve the positive result, strengthen the control until it can reproduce the candidate, freeze the next prediction, publish the failure, and refuse to open a confirmatory benchmark before the protocol is ready.
